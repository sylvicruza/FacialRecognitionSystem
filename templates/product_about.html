Choose how people check in

Start simple. Add faster and smarter attendance methods as your organisation grows.

Manual swipe

Populate members for an event, then swipe right for present or left for absent.

QR code

Members scan an event QR, or staff scan member QR codes at entry.

NFC

Use cards or tags for quick tap-in attendance on supported devices.

Facial recognition

Enterprise mobile attendance with location rules and controlled access.
